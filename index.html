The Chalet

For slow mornings and long evenings by the water

Tucked along Chemin de la Chute on the banks of the Coulonge River, Sérénité Ô Chalet is a four season cottage located 1.5 hours from Ottawa, in a picturesque and historic Mansfield-et-Pontefract. This timber-frame cottage was designed the way the great Norwegian hytte are — steep gables, warm wood for the winter nights, walls of glass that pull the forest and the water indoors, to enjoy all year long and not to forget your very own private skating rink.

The Chalet features three bedrooms sleep up to eight guests in comfort, with an open-concept living and dining area, a wraparound deck with sandy beach facing the water, and a 6 person wood-fired Cedar sauna for the cold-plunge ritual after a day outdoors.

To add adventure to your stay, the chalet is close to Chute De Cologne with an adventure park, via ferrata and zip lines.
